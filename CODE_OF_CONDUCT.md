# Code of Conduct

SafeNest is a community built around protecting children, so we hold ourselves to a high standard of respect.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, patient and constructive
- Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- Give and accept feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for children and for the community

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery of any kind
- Publishing others' private information
- Any content or discussion that could harm or exploit children

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors who do so repeatedly. Report violations privately to the maintainers using the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
