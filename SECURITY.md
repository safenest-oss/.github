# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report vulnerabilities in public issues.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the **Security** tab of the affected repository, then **Report a vulnerability**), or email: `CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE`

Please include a description, steps to reproduce and the affected version. We aim to respond within 7 days.

## Scope

Issues that could expose children's data, bypass a protection in a harmful way, or violate our [privacy principles](PRIVACY_PRINCIPLES.md) are treated as high priority.
